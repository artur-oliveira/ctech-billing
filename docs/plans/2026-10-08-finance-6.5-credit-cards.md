# Finance 6.5 — Credit cards (F5, statement closing in the job) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A person records a card, its purchases in installments, and sees each month's statement (*fatura*): the job closes it on the closing day into a payable bill, paying that bill moves cash, a purchase can be refunded (*estorno*) or its installments advanced (*antecipação*), and the DRE holds every purchase whole on its date.

**Architecture:** A card is a `liability` ledger account plus a `CARD#{id}` row in a new `cards` table that holds its closing/due days, paying account, the **open month** and the job's schedule key. Each installment is an `ITEM` row under its statement month; an open statement is just its items (no stored total, so a purchase never touches more than its own rows). Closing freezes the sum into a `STATEMENT#{month}` row and creates the statement bill in one transaction, guarded by the card's open month and a version counter that every purchase bumps. The statement bill has no recognition (the purchase already put the expense in the DRE); settling it clears the card instead of *payables*, with cash flow `card:{id}`.

**Tech Stack:** Go 1.27.2, Fiber v3, DynamoDB (api-commons Base); Next.js 16 static export, React 19, TanStack Query, `@aoctech/ui`, Tailwind 4, vitest + Testing Library.

**Spec:** [`docs/specs/2026-10-07-finance-erp-design.md`](../specs/2026-10-07-finance-erp-design.md) — § 3.2 (card rows of the posting table), § 3.4 (bill lifecycle, `origin = card_statement`, `payment_group`), § 3.6 (cards: allocation, refund, advance, closed statements never change), § 4 (`cards` table), § 5 step 3 (close statements), § 6.1 (verbs), § 7 F5/F8, § 10 (job re-runnable, installments sum). Builds on [6.4](2026-10-08-finance-6.4-reports.md) (flows: scope decision 8 reserved `card:{id}`).

## Scope decisions (read before executing)

1. **The card's settings live in `cards`, its money in the ledger.** `POST /cards` writes the ledger account (`class = liability`, no DRE group) and `S → CARD#{id}` in one `TransactWriteItems` (two tables, two items). The account id is the card id. Listing cards is one prefix Query on `S` in `cards` (spec § 4 gains this row; purchases and statements stay under `S#CARD#{id}` as specified).
2. **An open statement has no stored total.** Its total is the sum of its `ITEM` rows, read with the one prefix Query that also returns them. A purchase in 48× is then 48 item puts + 1 card update + 1 purchase row + ~8 ledger items ≈ 58, under the 100-item limit; a stored per-statement total would have doubled it past 100.
3. **The open month is the floor of every allocation.** The card row holds `open_month` (the first statement not yet closed). A purchase dated in a month already closed lands on the open statement (spec: "a late correction lands on the next open statement"). Every write that adds or removes items conditions on `open_month = :m` **and ADDs 1 to `version`**; closing conditions on `open_month = :m AND version = :v`. A purchase racing the job therefore either lands before the close's read (and is counted) or fails its condition and is retried by the client; it is never written into a frozen statement.
4. **Closing** (job or `POST /cards/:id/close`): sum the open month's items; put `STATEMENT#{m}` (if absent) with the frozen total, closing and due dates; if the total is positive, put the statement bill (deterministic id from `(space, card, month)`, so a re-run cannot create two); if negative (refunds exceeded charges), put a `carry` credit item on the next month; zero closes with no bill; move `open_month` to the next month and the schedule key to its closing date. A job that missed several closings closes them in order in one run.
5. **Dates.** Statement *m* closes on `min(closing_day, days in m)` of *m*. It is due on the first `due_day` (clamped) strictly after that closing date: in *m* when `due_day > closing day`, otherwise in *m + 1*. Business-day roll is out of scope (issuers vary; the person edits the bill's due date if theirs rolls).
6. **The statement bill is not an expense.** `origin = card_statement`, `origin_ref = "{card}#{yyyy-mm}"`, `category_id = card id`, `account_id = card's paying account`, `payment_group = "{card}#{yyyy-mm}"`. Creating it posts **nothing** to the ledger. Settling it posts card ↑ / cash ↓ with cash flow `card:{card}` (kind stays `settlement`, so "Desfazer pagamento" works unchanged). It cannot be canceled, and an edit cannot change its amount or category; due date, paying account, description and auto-settle stay editable.
7. **Refund** reverses the purchase transaction (dated today: the DRE loses it in the refund month), deletes the installments not yet billed, and puts one credit item on the open statement for those already billed. **Advance** deletes the installments after the open month and puts one item with their sum on the open month; no ledger change. Both are refused on a refunded purchase and are `finance.write`.
8. **Out of this plan:** opening debt on a card (a card that already owes from before the ledger), interest/IOF lines, card projection in F1, business-day roll of due dates, editing a purchase (refund and re-enter it).

```go
package finance

import (
	"errors"
	"fmt"

	"gopkg.aoctech.app/billing/api/internal/domain/brcal"
)

// ErrInvalidCard wraps every reason a card is refused.
var ErrInvalidCard = errors.New("invalid card")

// Card is a credit card's settings (spec § 3.6). Its money is a liability
// account in the ledger with the same id; this is when its statements close,
// when they are due, and which account pays them.
type Card struct {
	ID              string
	ClosingDay      int
	DueDay          int
	PayingAccountID string
	// OpenMonth is the first statement not yet closed: every installment lands
	// on it or later, so a closed statement is never changed.
	OpenMonth Month
}

func (c Card) Validate() error {
	fail := func(format string, a ...any) error {
		return fmt.Errorf("%w: "+format, append([]any{ErrInvalidCard}, a...)...)
	}
	switch {
	case c.ClosingDay < 1 || c.ClosingDay > 31:
		return fail("closing day must be 1..31")
	case c.DueDay < 1 || c.DueDay > 31:
		return fail("due day must be 1..31")
	case c.PayingAccountID == "":
		return fail("the paying account is required")
	case c.OpenMonth == (Month{}):
		return fail("the open month is required")
	}
	return nil
}

func clampDay(m Month, day int) brcal.Date {
	return brcal.New(m.Year, m.Month, min(day, brcal.DaysInMonth(m.Year, m.Month)))
}

// ClosingDate is the day statement m closes: its closing day, clamped to the
// month's last day (a card closing on the 31st closes on Feb 28).
func ClosingDate(m Month, closingDay int) brcal.Date { return clampDay(m, closingDay) }

// DueDate is the first due day strictly after the statement's closing date:
// in the same month when the due day comes after the closing day, otherwise in
// the next one.
func DueDate(m Month, closingDay, dueDay int) brcal.Date {
	if d := clampDay(m, dueDay); d.After(ClosingDate(m, closingDay)) {
		return d
	}
	return clampDay(m.Add(1), dueDay)
}

// CardFlow is the cash-flow attribution of paying a card's statement (6.4
// scope decision 8): one line per card in the cash flow.
func CardFlow(cardID string) string { return "card:" + cardID }

// StatementRef names one statement of one card: the statement bill's
// origin_ref and payment_group.
func StatementRef(cardID string, m Month) string { return cardID + "#" + m.String() }
```

```go
// cardBump is the guard every write that adds or removes items carries: the
// card's open month is still the one the write allocated against, and its
// version moves, so a close that read the items before this write fails its own
// condition instead of freezing a total without it (scope decision 3).
func (r *CardRepository) cardBump(sp space.ResolvedSpace, cardID string, open finance.Month, now time.Time) types.TransactWriteItem {
	sk := CardSK(cardID)
	return r.cards.BuildRawUpdateTxItem(sp.PK(), &sk, "ADD version :one SET updated_at = :now", "open_month = :m", nil,
		map[string]types.AttributeValue{":one": numberValue(1), ":m": str(open.String()), ":now": str(now.UTC().Format(time.RFC3339Nano))})
}
```

```go
// CloseAt closes read.OpenMonth: one transaction freezes the total, creates the
// statement bill (positive) or carries the credit (negative), and moves the open
// month — guarded by the open month AND the version read, so a purchase written
// after the items were summed makes this fail with ErrCardMoved instead of
// being left out of a frozen total.
func (r *CardRepository) CloseAt(ctx context.Context, sp space.ResolvedSpace, read CardRow, today brcal.Date, now time.Time) error {
	m := read.OpenMonth
	s, err := r.GetStatement(ctx, sp, read.ID, m)
	if err != nil {
		return err
	}
	closing, due := finance.ClosingDate(m, read.ClosingDay), finance.DueDate(m, read.ClosingDay, read.DueDay)
	var items []types.TransactWriteItem
	billID := ""
	switch {
	case s.Total > 0:
		billID = idempotentID(sp, "statement", finance.StatementRef(read.ID, m))
		bill, err := r.bills.statementBillItem(sp, finance.Bill{
			ID: billID, Direction: finance.Payable, Amount: s.Total, AccountID: read.PayingAccountID, CategoryID: read.ID,
			Description: "Fatura " + read.Name + " " + m.String(), Competence: closing, Due: due,
			Status: finance.BillForecast, Origin: finance.OriginCardStatement,
			OriginRef: finance.StatementRef(read.ID, m), PaymentGroup: finance.StatementRef(read.ID, m),
		}, now)
		if err != nil {
			return err
		}
		items = append(items, bill)
	case s.Total < 0:
		carry, err := Encode(itemRow{
			keys: newKeys(CardPK(sp, read.ID), ItemSK(m.Add(1), "carry", m.String()), RetentionPermanent, now),
			PurchaseID: "carry", Description: "Crédito da fatura anterior", Date: closing.String(), Kind: "carry", Amount: int64(s.Total),
		})
		if err != nil {
			return err
		}
		items = append(items, r.cards.BuildPutTxItemIfAbsent(carry))
	}
	row, err := Encode(statementRow{
		keys: newKeys(CardPK(sp, read.ID), StatementSK(m), RetentionPermanent, now),
		Total: int64(s.Total), ClosedOn: today.String(), BillID: billID,
	})
	if err != nil {
		return err
	}
	items = append(items, r.cards.BuildPutTxItemIfAbsent(row))
	next := m.Add(1)
	sk := CardSK(read.ID)
	items = append(items, r.cards.BuildRawUpdateTxItem(sp.PK(), &sk,
		"SET open_month = :next, schedule_sk = :ssk, updated_at = :now", "open_month = :m AND version = :v", nil,
		map[string]types.AttributeValue{
			":next": str(next.String()), ":m": str(m.String()), ":v": numberValue(read.Version),
			":ssk": str(ScheduleSK(finance.ClosingDate(next, read.ClosingDay), sp.Owner(), read.ID)),
			":now": str(now.UTC().Format(time.RFC3339Nano)),
		}))
	if err := r.cards.TransactWrite(ctx, items); err != nil {
		if onlyConditionFailed(err) {
			return ErrCardMoved
		}
		return err
	}
	return nil
}
```

`statementRow{keys; Total int64 "total"; ClosedOn string "closed_on"; BillID string "bill_id,omitempty"}`.

`CloseDue`: loop: `GetCard`; if `ClosingDate(card.OpenMonth, card.ClosingDay)` is after `today`, stop; `CloseAt`; on `ErrCardMoved` re-read and retry the same month at most 3 times; count closes. `CloseNow`: `space.Write`; `GetCard`; `CloseAt(ctx, sp, card, brcal.FromTime(now), now)`; return `GetStatement` of the closed month. `DueToClose`: `scanSchedule(ctx, r.cards, ClosePK(livemode), livemode, today, limit)` → `DueCard{Space, CardID: e.ID}`.

## Out of this plan (and why)

- **Opening debt on a card.** `PostOpeningBalance` only takes assets (6.4); a card that already owes needs a liability opening and a first statement seeded from it — its own small change once someone asks.
- **Interest, IOF, annual fee lines.** They arrive on the issuer's statement; 6.6's import is where they come in.
- **Card installments in F1's projection.** The projection reads open bills and recurrences; future statements are known and could be added, but nothing in 6.5's screens needs it.
- **Business-day roll of the due date.** Issuers differ; the statement bill's due date is editable.
